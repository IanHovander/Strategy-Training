The strategist waits with the other team reps in front of the field: 5026 finished eleventh, so they need a captain to pick them. MC Robin skates in on inline skates to explain how picking works, then the picks begin. Watch it, then walk the field and talk to the alliances about how it went. The cut scene ends with you walking off the field through the gate, so you start on the left half of the floor in front of it; the way out is straight on to the right, through the gap in the belt barrier below the audience display, under the EXIT sign (here it brings you back to the start; in the game it's on to Worlds).

How it's made

  • MC Robin: a new character on the same rig as everyone else: a hot-pink blazer (the only magenta in the arena; no team wears it) over a black tee, navy slacks, a gold bow tie, dark shades with a glint, short black curls and a grin. Her head is drawn by hand for five directions and mirrored for the other three. She holds a handheld mic in her right hand.
  • On skates: white boots on steel frames with three yellow wheels each, so she stands a few pixels taller than anyone on foot. The rig has four new poses for her: the glide (one skate pushes out to the side and back while the other glides under her, leaning in, arms swinging wide), a stop with flair after a spin, the mic up to her mouth, and the mic held out to whoever's answering. Her feet step with the distance she rolls, the same as everyone's walk.
  • The set: the arena from the walkabout, its own shared art: the field with its walls, HABs, cargo ship and rockets, and the stands and crowd above (they cheer together when Robin asks). The robots are gone. Across the front of the field are eight yellow numbered signs on posts, 1 at the blue end to 8 at the red end, built like the QUEUE sign and held up just behind each alliance's heads. (The line runs from the blue end, 1, to the red end, 8, the way the field reads left to right.) The mic stands on a stand in the middle: the same steel-headed mic as Robin's, its light round head easy to pick out against the cargo ship's dark bays, and the near wall has a gate with its chain unhooked.
  • In front of the field: the arena's concrete carries on below the field, so the world is 208 rows deep. On it are a taped waiting line, a TEAM REPS sign and a belt barrier on posts like the driver stations'. Past the red end, the audience display stands on two truss legs. It fills in as the captains come out and each pick is made, shows the pick clock in red and lists all eight backups at the bottom. Below it is the way out, built like the arena's: the barrier turns up the right-hand side to the display, with a gap in it and a short chute out past the edge, its lane taped on the floor. A red EXIT sign hangs from a tall pole on the gap's far post, out over the chute, so she walks out under it (it's left out of the cut scene's shots of the display, which are about the alliances).
  • The reps: a student from each of the 33 teams, built like the crowd in the stands (one of the five hand-drawn heads, the cast's skins and hair), each in their own team's colours: 4000 orange, 2468 green, 3000 blue, 7777 yellow with black jeans, 8086 teal, 9152 lime, and so on, some in hoodies and caps. Teal, lime and heather grey are new three-tone ramps. Nobody else wears red, so 5026's tee stands out among them. Each alliance stands as a group of three: the captain at the sign, the two picks close behind on either side. Priya, the queuer, and Sam, the field volunteer, are the arena's own. The head referee, in black-and-white stripes, waits by the near wall past the end of the line to brief the captains.
  • The rules, from the 2019 manual (section 12.7): the top eight seeds become the alliance captains, each represented by one student. Round one picks from Alliance One to Eight, and round two from Eight back to One, so each alliance ends up with three teams. A captain can only invite a team ranked below them. A team that declines an invitation can't be picked by anyone else and can't be a backup. When a captain accepts another captain's invitation, every captain below moves up a spot and the next team in the rankings becomes captain 8. The best-ranked teams left over become backups, called in by rank. At the Championship, where alliances have four teams, a third round goes from Alliance One to Eight again, and there are no backups.
  • The way it's run today: the pick clock (45 seconds a pick in round one, 90 in round two, started by the MC's "you are on the clock"), the MC's "would you prefer to join or form your own alliance?" when a captain invites another captain, "congratulations!", and the send-off ("pick an alliance to cheer on", "bring your gracious professionalism") all come from FIRST's current alliance selection script. Invitations use the usual wording: "Team 3000 respectfully invites Team 5026 to join our alliance." The teams, the event and the MC are made up.
  • The cut scene: it opens close on the crowd, cuts to the arena, the stands full over the empty line of signs, and pans along the line from 1 to 8, then tilts down to the waiting reps. Robin skates in through the gate and lands a spin by the mic. She welcomes everyone, thanks the volunteers and sponsors, and explains the rules while she skates the order the picks go in: sign 1 to sign 8 for round one, and back from 8 to 1 for round two. A captain can invite any team ranked below them, even another captain. Then she calls the captains out by seed, and each runs out to their sign as she calls them. Alliance One invites 2468, the number two seed, who accepts, so the captains below shuffle up a spot and 9152 runs on as the new number eight. Then Alliance Two invites 5026: Robin rolls out through the gate and holds out the mic, the strategist steps out of the crowd to answer, Robin congratulates her, and she runs on to stand with her captain. (At a real event the invited team's rep walks up to the MC's mic on the field; Robin skates it out instead, and owns up to it if you catch her.) A fade skips to the end of round two, and the whole arena stands to applaud every team. Then the strategist walks off the field through the gate: the picks are free to go, and the captains stay for the head referee's briefing. It's built like the intro, with eased pans, hard cuts and close-ups at exactly twice the size.
  • Who you can talk to: Lena, your captain (what makes a good pick, playoff stations); Rex, your second pick (reliability); Maya and Theo, who made the captain-picks-captain move (and why a captain can only invite teams ranked below them); Kit, whose first invite was turned down; Ike, who moved up from ninth; Zoe, whose team declined and can't be picked now; Gabe, the top backup; Priya, who checks the backups in; Sam at the gate; Dale, the head referee (the playoff briefing, timeouts, backups and the Championship's third round); and Robin, who stops skating when you come over.
