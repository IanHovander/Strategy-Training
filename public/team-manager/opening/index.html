Blue Banner: the opening

The first minute of the game. Saturday, 12:38 PM, alliance selection. Her team is seed 4, an alliance captain, and the senior strategist who was meant to make the pick has just texted in sick. Down on the floor with the team reps, the co-captain hands her the pick list, and Norbert for luck. Then MC Robin calls her up to the field, and the 45-second pick clock starts. The clock counts down over the scouting tablet: two teams to choose from, and one thing to notice.

The shots

  1. Black, 0.7 s: the crowd's noise rising, drawn as a line of sound across the dark, with phone screens and camera flashes up in the stands.
  2. Her phone, 2.4 s: full screen, in her hand. Her "WHERE ARE YOU??" is still unanswered, then the typing dots, then "SICK. CAN'T COME." and "YOU'VE GOT THIS.", the phone buzzing as each arrives. The clock on the phone says 12:37.
  3. The card, about 3 s: ALLIANCE SELECTION, SATURDAY 12:38 PM, the minute ticking over from 12:37, with MC Robin over the PA: "WELCOME TO ALLIANCE SELECTION!"
  4. The arena, 1.2 s: wide from the floor in front of the field, the house lights down. The captains stand in their signs' spotlights, sign 4's lit with nobody in it, and Robin is by the mic. Riley and the strategist wait with the other team reps, their backs to you.
  5. The co-captain, 1.4 s, silent: close on the two of them, the rest of the arena's lights down to a glimmer. Riley turns from the field to her, Norbert hugged to their chest, and she turns to Riley. This shot holds, so A can't skip it.
  6. The handoff: "THE LIST IS YOURS." Riley holds Norbert out: "AND TAKE HIM. HE'S BEEN TO EVERY EVENT SINCE 2014." Then a cut to their hands at twice the size as Norbert passes from Riley's red sleeves to her, and his 26 ear glints once. This shot holds too.
  7. Her: close on her with Norbert in her arms: "...OKAY." From here on, Norbert sits in the top left corner with his battery voltage.
  8. Her call, about 2 s: the first three alliances have picked meanwhile. Robin calls "5026, COME ON UP!" over the audience display, which shows alliances 1 to 3 filled, row 4 lit and the clock at 0:45. Then a cut to her walking up through the gate to the field, with Riley watching her go.
  9. The pick: a hard cut to the scouting tablet. The clock slams in at 0:45 and starts counting as soon as the pick is hers. The stick (or the arrow keys) moves between the two cards, A picks, and the invite asks INVITE or BACK. Then the bet log: a right pick goes on to TO BE CONTINUED; a wrong one shows why and rewinds the clock, Norbert's ear glowing.

How it's made

  • The shot list is the design's (docs/blue-banner-design.md, section 5, "Building the opening"), cut like the intro: hard cuts between shots, an eased pan in the wide shot, close-ups at exactly twice the size, film bars throughout, and every frame drawn from the cut scene's clock. With the words turning by themselves, it runs 21.6 seconds to the pick (measured). A hurries the words.
  • The arena is alliance selection's, borrowed as it is: its set, its reps, MC Robin on her skates, its layout and its lights. Seeds 1 to 3 and 5 to 8 stand at their signs along the arc, each in a spotlight. Everyone else waits on the floor in front of the field. The strategist is in her place in the front corner by the gate, with Riley beside her, between her and the crowd. Once the first three alliances have picked, their first picks stand behind their captains.
  • Riley is a new character on the walker rig. A senior in the team's red hoodie with the panther badge, black jeans, and a hand-drawn head: a tall curly top over short sides, held back by a white sweatband, so Riley stands out from her, the teammate and every rep. Riley hugs Norbert in the walker's clap pose and holds him out in the drive pose.
  • Norbert is drawn three ways. In someone's arms he's a new 12x14 sprite, drawn by hand from the 16x19 corner one. In the handoff he's the full 36x44 sprite at twice the size. After the handoff he's in the corner, with 12.4V beside him: enough charge to rewind a few minutes, not yet enough to reach Thursday (12.9V comes after the semifinal).
  • Her phone is a dark-mode texting screen, with her fingers round its edge and her thumb on the side. The words use the game's 5x7 letters.
  • The pick is the scouting tablet (pick.js, tablet.js, teams.js): the clock and its draining bar, her robot pinned for reference, and two cards, each with the team number, a big average, one note and its ten matches as boxes. 7461 shows 41, the biggest number on the screen, but its three Saturday boxes are blank: it sat dead, and the app's average skips blanks. 6834 shows 31 and never broke. The lesson, in one line: look at the matches, not the average.

The scene is in this folder: cutscene.js (the shots), cocaptain.js, phone.js, handoff.js, norbert-held.js, pick.js, tablet.js, teams.js and scene.js. It borrows alliance selection's set.js, people.js, mc.js and layout.js, and runs on the shared stage.js.
