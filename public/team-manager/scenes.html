Blue Banner

A game about being a rookie strategist at a robotics regional. It plays from the title screen to the end of the first event: thrown in at alliance selection with one page of numbers, wound back to Thursday by the team's clockwork penguin, then three days to walk the pits, watch robots, plan your own matches and fill a notebook, before alliance selection and the playoffs come round again.

Play the game

The opening reel and the title screen, then NEW SEASON (or CONTINUE, if you've played before on this device). Hold your phone sideways: tap what you want to press, tap the floor to walk there, tap someone to talk to them; the dot in the bottom right corner steers. On a computer: WASD or the arrow keys, Shift to run, Space for A, B for the notebook, M for the sound.

The opening

Saturday, 12:38 PM. The senior strategist is out sick, the co-captain hands her the pick list and Norbert, and she's on the clock: every team that's left on the tablet, 45 seconds, and whoever she invites, she's got. Then the playoffs with that alliance, for real.

Thursday: the pits

All 24 pits in one long hall. Every team will tell you what their robot does, how it's running and who impressed them. Some of it's true.

Friday: qualification

Ten rounds. Before each of her matches: the stands, to watch a robot, or the pits. Then the plan for her own alliance, the match on the field, and the call at 0:30. (Starts at round 1; add &round=6 to the address for Friday's last.)

Saturday: the boss fight

Alliance selection with the event's real rankings and whatever's in the notebook, then the playoffs, best two of three. Starts with an event played on the drive coach's plans and a notebook half filled in.

The places, as they were first built

Each opens on its own cut scene, then hands you the strategist to walk around and talk to people; on its own page, the phone beside the big screen steers her. Their stories are filler from before the game: the game uses the van, the arena and alliance selection's arena as its own places.

The scene tour

All of these in turn, full screen, coming in on the left of each and leaving on the right.

Team meeting

The whole team in a circle on the back lawn, in team merch, the shop door rolled up behind them. One co-captain gives the shout-outs, the other the updates on what's coming, then Iron Panthers on three.

Practice field

Half a 2019 field built from plywood on carpet in the school cafeteria, late sun pouring through the tall windows, the drive team running timed hatch cycles while a mentor calls the times.

The van

Eight in the van at dawn, towing the trailer: the mentor driving, a student navigating, two rows of three. The camera scans the cabin front to back and the strategist and the teammate talk; then you pick a seat to talk to anyone.

The pits

Eight team pits along the hall at the regional. Crews kneel at their robots with drills, deploy code and carry batteries; the pits whose robot is away say IN QUEUE or ON FIELD. 5026's robot rolls out to the queue after the strategist gives the drive team the plan.

The arena

The walkway between the bleachers and the 2019 field, with the queue and the scoring table past the ends. Real simulated matches one after another, and the whole changeover between them: robots off and on through the gates, the field reset, the emcee's introductions.

Alliance selection

The eight captains on their numbered spots across the field, the mic in the middle, every other team's rep waiting outside. MC Robin skates in, explains the picks the way FIRST's process does, and two picks play out, the second for 5026.

World Championship

Eight division fields side by side in Houston's hall, every one drawn by the arena's own field code, each match on its own clock. The camera glides the length of the hall to Newton, 5026's division.

Labs

Match lab

A real 2019 match, simulated and played out on the arena's field: six robots cycling, defending, breaking down and climbing, every point going in on the field as the scoreboard ticks. Set the plan, play it at any speed, scrub back and forth, and see every kind of robot.

Time travel lab

Eight ways the strategist could move through time, on the real alliance selection cut scene, to choose the rewind the game uses.
