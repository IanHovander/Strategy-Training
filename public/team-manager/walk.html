How she's made

  • One rig, every angle: she's built as a small 3D figure (rounded shapes for her head, hair, body and limbs), turned to each of the eight directions and drawn to pixels in her own colours, three tones each, with an ink outline. Her eyes and smile are placed as single pixels so they stay crisp.
  • Walking: eight frames a stride, arms swinging against her legs, the board hanging from her left hand.
  • Running: eight frames, leaning in, knees high, arms pumping, the board tucked under her arm so it doesn't flap.
  • No sliding feet: the animation steps with the distance she covers, so her stride matches her speed.

The walking sprite lives in walker.js; the full-height portrait on the title screen is hero.js.
