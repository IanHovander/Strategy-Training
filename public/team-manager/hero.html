Blue Banner: the hero

A full-height sprite for the main character. It is drawn pixel by pixel at the screen's own resolution instead of the game's usual double-size pixels, which leaves room for a real face. The hair is the open question, so there are three to choose from; everything else is shared.

What to look at

  • Face: straight dark brows, open eyes with a brown iris and a lash line, a nose drawn with shadow, an easy closed smile, and a jaw that narrows to the chin.
  • Hair: black with blue-grey highlights that follow the strands. Curtain bangs and side-swept are common on Asian teens; the ponytail is tied back the way the pit safety rules ask.
  • Stance: weight on one leg with the other knee relaxed, one hand in a pocket with the elbow out, and the board carried by its clip.
  • Clothes: the red 5026 team shirt with sleeves and a crease where the pocket hand pulls it, plain jeans with a fly and pockets, and white sneakers.
  • The board: the 2019 field (blue HAB left, red HAB right, the cargo ship in the middle, rockets on the walls), a blue cycle over the cargo ship and a red X where the defender waits. The marker swings on its cord.

The sprite lives in hero.js. Nothing in the intro uses it yet; the title screen still shows the current coach until a hairstyle is picked.
