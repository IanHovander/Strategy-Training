Blue Banner: the hero

A full-height sprite for the main character. It is drawn pixel by pixel at the screen's own resolution instead of the game's usual double-size pixels, which leaves room for a real face.

What to look at

  • Face: turned toward the robots with her eyes back on you, and a small smile that's all the way real: corners up, a glimpse of teeth, cheeks lifted with a little blush.
  • Hair: shoulder length and near black with faint brown glints, parted at the side and swept across her forehead. It rests on her near shoulder and falls behind the other, with a soft wave.
  • Stance: weight on her near leg, so that hip pops out under her hand and the other shoulder rides higher. The other leg is relaxed, and her feet point the way she's turned.
  • Clothes: the red 5026 team shirt, fitted, with the front tucked into high-waisted jeans at the button. Rolled cuffs show one yellow sock and one blue, above canvas sneakers.
  • The board: hanging from her far hand and tilted away from her side, wiped clean with no plan on it yet. The marker swings under it on its cord.

The sprite lives in hero.js, and the intro's title screen uses it.
