    What to look for

    • Your robot is pinned at the top, a card like the others. Read the chips straight down and you'll see who does what you do.
    • The average is big and gold. It's the tempting number, and the trap: 7461's 41 looks like the pick.
    • COMPARE puts you side by side: 30 + 41 = 71 on paper, the chips you share marked BOTH (two robots, one Level 3 platform), and TOGETHER left as "?" until you know. After the van it says about 51.
    • The "?" cards show a face: whoever on the team knows the answer. That's who you go and find in the van.
    • After Friday's scouting, every team gets a sparkline of its ten matches. 6417's jump after match 5 is the whole lesson in one picture. Sort by LAST 5 and they move to the top.
    • The pick gets a stamp in the bet log: ✓, ~ or ✗ on what the tablet knew, with the chance of reaching the final it gave you and the best you could have had. WIND BACK is Norbert's rewind.
