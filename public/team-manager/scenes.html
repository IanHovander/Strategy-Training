Blue Banner: scenes

Every place in the season so far. Each one opens on a cut scene, then hands you the strategist to walk around and talk to people, steered from the phone beside the big screen.

The pits

A row of team pits at an event: some crews working on their robots, other pits empty while their robot is in the queue or out on the field.

Alliance selection

The eight alliance captains out on the field, the team representatives waiting by it, and the MC on roller blades explaining how picking works.

Team meeting

The team in a circle on the grass outside, in team merch, while the co-captains give shout-outs and say what's coming up.

The van

Eight on the road to the event: a mentor driving, a student navigating, two rows of three behind. The camera runs front to back, then you talk.

Practice field

Half a field built in the school cafeteria, with sunlight coming through the windows.

World Championship

Eight division fields side by side in one hall, all built from the same field art as the arena.

The arena

The walkway between the bleachers and the 2019 field, with the queue and the scoring table past the ends.

The intro

The opening sizzle reel and the title screen.
