Blue Banner: time travel

Four ways the strategist could move through time, on the real alliance selection cut scene. Let it play, then press the red button (or Space, or tap the picture) to rewind to "Welcome to alliance selection!" or jump forward to the end of round two. Rewinds turn counterclockwise and forward jumps clockwise. Each one looks best when she's on screen: she's the rep in the red tee.

The four looks

1 STOP: everything stops but you
A tick, and the world freezes mid-word and drains to the cold blue of stopped time. Only she keeps her colour, and she's the only one who moves: she looks right, left, then straight at you. Norbert's 26 ear lights up. Then the world runs backwards around her, leaving trails, with her still in colour, and the colour floods back where she lands.
2 SWIRL: the vortex
The picture twists into a vortex around her, like an old console's battle swirl: counterclockwise to go back, clockwise to go forward. The frames run backwards (or on) inside it, a light opens in the middle, a white flash, and it unwinds into where she lands.
3 TAPE: rewinding the tape
A tape rewinding: a clunk, torn rows rolling down the screen, the colours split, REW and the event clock counting back in the corner, the words un-typing. A clunk to stop, then STOP flashes. Funny and easy to read, but it says "video", not "time".
4 ALL: the pick for the opening
STOP, then the vortex opens around her while she stands still, in colour, in the middle of the turning world. The light swallows the picture, a clock card spins its hands back to 12:38 while the time rolls down, and the world unwinds back into colour at the start.

How it works

  • A real rewind: every scene draws each frame as a pure function of time, so going back plays the actual cut scene backwards. While it plays, the lab writes down every frame's moment (beat, time and the words on screen) and draws them again last to first. The words un-type as it goes.
  • Only her: each frame is drawn twice, with her and without her; the pixels that differ are her. That's how the world stops and drains of colour while she doesn't, and how she stands still in the middle of the vortex.
  • Norbert: top left, 16 by 19 pixels, drawn down from the full sprite, with the voltage of the battery inside him as the team's battery tester would read it. His red 26 ear lights up as time starts to move.
  • Sound: made on the spot with Web Audio: a tick and a hum when time stops, a rushing wind that rises going forward and falls going back, a tape whirr and clunk, and a chime on arrival.
  • From the address bar: ?effect=1 to 4, ?dir=forward, ?auto=SECONDS to jump by itself, ?mute.

The lab is in this folder: lab.js (playing the cut scene, recording it and the four looks), warp.js (the picture effects) and sound.js. Norbert is ../norbert.js. It plays alliance selection's own cut scene (../alliance-selection/).
